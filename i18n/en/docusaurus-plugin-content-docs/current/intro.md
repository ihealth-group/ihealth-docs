# Welcome to the Documentation

## About iHealth {#sobre-a-ihealth}

**iHealth** is a DoctorAssistant.ai group company that uses artificial intelligence to turn clinical data into applied intelligence. We structure and analyze electronic health record data to deliver solutions for the pharmaceutical industry, hospitals and health insurers.

## Our Mission {#nossa-missão}

To turn clinical data into strategic intelligence through advanced technology, connecting real-world information to clinical and strategic decisions in an ethical, secure and intelligent way.

## Collaborative Data Lake {#data-lake-colaborativo}

Our collaborative data lake brings together highly granular clinical data from Brazilian hospitals in every region of the country. With it, we deliver solutions that change how healthcare is managed and researched.

## Available Documentation {#documentação-disponível}

### iHealth Platform {#plataforma-ihealth}

The **iHealth Platform** reads progress notes, reports, discharge summaries and consultation notes with artificial intelligence models and puts the structured content within reach in clinical language, so you can build cohorts, explore populations and support analyses.

**Platform access:** [plataforma.ihealthgroup.tec.br](https://plataforma.ihealthgroup.tec.br/)

- **Structured search** over clinical text, with categories, contexts and relations between entities
- **General dashboard** of the database available for research
- **Funnel analysis** for step-by-step eligibility criteria
- **Clinical protocols** to follow and share cohorts over time
- **RWE / RWD studies**, feasibility and recruitment

[**See the Platform documentation →**](./platform/welcome.md)

### Clinical Data Extraction {#extração-de-dados-clínicos}

**Clinical Data Extraction** delivers structured, anonymized data from our clinical database, selected according to criteria defined by the client (diagnoses, medications, procedures, etc.), for analysis by data scientists and data analysts.

- **Structured, anonymized** clinical data
- **CSV and JSONL formats**, delivered in batches
- **Clinical context preserved**, including the relations between entities
- **Structured fields** for biomarkers and lab tests
- **Code examples** to load, validate and analyze the data

[**See the Extraction documentation →**](./clinical-data-extraction/intro)
